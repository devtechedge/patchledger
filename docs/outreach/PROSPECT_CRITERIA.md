# Prospect Criteria & Sourcing

## Must have (score 2+ to qualify)

1. Dev agency / studio / consultancy (2-30 people) OR boutique SaaS team (<30) - not freelancers with one personal project, not enterprises.
2. US / UK / EU based or primarily serving those markets (English communication).
3. Evidence of at least one: enterprise/regulated clients (fintech, health, legal, gov-adjacent case studies), public maintenance/care retainer offering, or public dependency-security commentary.
4. Reachable: public email, LinkedIn, contact form, or community-DMable.

## Nice to have

- Public client-facing maintenance reports in their portfolio (they already try to prove work).
- Job posts mentioning security/compliance duties.
- Recent pen-test or SOC 2 mentions on their site.

## Sourcing channels (no scraping private data, no spam)

1. Community participants: r/devsecops, r/agency, r/ExperiencedDevs threads about Dependabot/questionnaire pain (public usernames -> public profiles -> public contact info only).
2. Directories: Clutch dev-agency listings with enterprise clients; thewp.world-style agency directories for mixed-stack shops; Indie Hackers founders selling B2B.
3. Pen-test firm public client lists and agency portfolios showing regulated clients.
4. Inbound: landing page + sample report link in one value-first community post (after approval).

## Not prospects

WordPress-only maintenance shops (Patchstack already owns that niche), solo freelancers without retainers, agencies explicitly serving only local SMBs (evidence: those clients rarely ask), enterprises with security teams.
